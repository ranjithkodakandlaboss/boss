## Gaps that were not filled by invention

- No open GitHub issue is scoped to Customer App foundation. Domain frontend issues were not expanded.
- `11_COMMON_AGENT_CONTEXT/02_DOMAIN_OWNERSHIP_AND_BOUNDARIES.md` is cited by the working agreement and is not in the repository.
- The Customer App lives in `src/customer-app`. Platform Admin UI in PR #119 keeps `src/frontend`.
- Frontend stories require a done backend story and the generated client at `src/customer-app/src/api/client.ts`. This foundation does not generate that client and does not call domain APIs.
- Business-option dropdowns are not hard-coded. Selects show that options are not loaded from `/api/v1/config/options/{category}`.
- FD-39 still blocks statutory invoice finalisation. FD-46 and FD-50 do not block this shell.
