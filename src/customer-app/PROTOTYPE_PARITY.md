# Customer App prototype parity

Prototype: `github.com/ranjithkodakandla/EOSS` branch `visual-experience-transformation`, commit `fb1f6159e70769975c135ec50e2532f2f6f3205f`.

Base: `claude/affectionate-cori-xt092x`.

A screen is complete only when its layout, copy, and interactions match the prototype and its records come from the customer API. Sales & Order screens now render the approved prototype components from `src/sales/proto` and read and write the customer API. They are not marked complete until the running DEV application has been compared with the prototype in the browser. Other screens in this checklist are not complete.

## Sales decisions recorded against the approved contracts

- Customer PO status is the physical state machine: `open`, `on_hold`, `partial`, `fulfilled`, `cancelled`. The OpenAPI feasibility statuses are issue #46 and are not stored.
- Prototype demo statuses such as RFQ `quoted` / `converted` and quotation `submitted` are shown with the contract statuses `draft` / `sent` / `closed` / `cancelled` and `draft` / `sent` / `accepted`.
- Accepting a sent or negotiating quotation records `OWNER_RECORDED`, the acting user and time, and creates one Customer PO. A repeated acceptance returns that PO. Decline and no response do not create a PO.
- "Submit to Customer" sets the quotation to `sent`. BOSS does not send email or WhatsApp for quotations. The PDF download is the document the owner sends outside BOSS.
- Direct Customer PO entry without an accepted quotation, PO amendment after execution, RFQ document upload, and the downstream order-journey stages are outside this release.

Quotation QT-20261117-B4857772 rev 1 was sent and downloaded as a PDF, then revised. Rev 1 stayed superseded. Rev 2 was accepted with owner identity, timestamp, confirmation note, and customer reference NW-PO-441, creating CPO-20261117-D97F131E. Repeating acceptance returned that same PO. A declined quotation and an unanswered sent quotation created no PO. The PO note was edited, the accepted unit price stayed ₹1,250, and hold/release worked. A separate customer was deactivated and remained readable. The records were still present after logout and login. Layout gaps below stay Partial.
